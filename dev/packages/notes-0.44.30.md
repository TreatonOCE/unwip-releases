- LitRPG ledger (Revise panel or Ctrl+K, with LitRPG tools on): reads every [system message] in the book and follows each stat chapter by chapter, with a small graph each. It flags stats that go down with nothing to explain it, or up with no level-up or [+2 Strength] in between, and levels that skip. It also lists every skill, spell, item, title, class and quest with the chapter it first appears in (quests show when they're complete), and notes anything mentioned before it's acquired. Click any of it to jump there.
- Build › Character sheet: a LitRPG status screen. Stats and bars (HP, MP…) with how much they grow each level, an XP curve (Steady, Climbing or Steep) with a level-by-level table, and a status screen at any level in four styles. Copy it into the book as [bracketed] lines the ledger can follow.
- E-books: an option (Settings › LitRPG tools) to make [system messages] real boxes in EPUB and Kindle. Word and print keep plain brackets.
