- Three levels instead of two: Focus, Detail and Lore (the buttons above your chapter list). Focus is a clean page. Detail adds the Outline, Corkboard, Moodboard, Timeline, status labels and synopses, and Build in the top bar. Lore adds character sheets. Switching only hides and shows things; nothing in your book changes, and Ctrl+K still finds every tool at any level. The main screen's Write, Play and Build tabs stay as they are.
- Character sheets (Lore): open a character from your Characters folder and click Character sheet (top left of the page). Put gear in the slots around the picture, each from the chapter you pick, with a rarity. The timeline at the top steps through the chapters where something changed, so you can look back at what they had and their stats at any point. Stats, skills and items come from the [system messages] in your chapters (the same reading as the LitRPG ledger); a block starting [Status: Name] keeps stats for that character. Pick Figure 1, Figure 2 or your own picture.
- "Copy for Docs" and its arrow are now one Export button. The menu is grouped (Copy and paste, Check it, Publish, Share, Take it elsewhere), one line per item with its icon, and the explanation shows at the bottom for the item you point at. Every option is still there, No hard feelings included. Ctrl+Shift+C still copies for Docs straight away.
- The top search button is now "Go to or do" (Ctrl+K), with its own icon and the shortcut written on it: chapters, characters, tools and settings. The magnifying glass at the bottom of the chapter list is the one for finding words in your book.
- Fixed: the Body / Heading box disappeared from the toolbar in Detailed when the window wasn't wide enough. It now stays; less important buttons tuck into ⋯ first.
- Revise's own switch now says Main checks / All checks, so it isn't confused with the new levels.
- The main screen has the same Focus / Detail / Lore switch, on the left. Play and Build unlock at Detail; new magic systems, rune alphabets, sigils and insignia are offered at Lore (ones you've already made always show). Phones go up to Detail; Lore needs a bigger screen.
- Add to a character sheet while you write: select an item's name, or a whole [Item Obtained: …] line, and click Add to sheet on the little bar (Lore), or right-click › Add to a character sheet on a PC or Mac. Pick the character, slot and rarity; it starts from the chapter you're in.
- Character sheets sync between devices. If gear was added on two devices at once, Compare and bring in keeps both, and asks you when the same slot was changed for the same chapter.
- The website's demo has the same three-level switch, with a Character sheets screen under Lore.
