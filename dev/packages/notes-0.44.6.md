Unwip has a new home: unwip.app.
- The web version (iPhone, iPad and browsers) now lives at unwip.app. The old address keeps working for now, with a "Move my books" button at the top of the library that brings every book and setting across in a moment. Nothing at the old address is deleted.
- Using Unwip from your Home Screen? Save a moving file at the old address, add unwip.app to your Home Screen, then choose "Bring books from the old Unwip" there.
- The PC and Mac apps aren't affected.
