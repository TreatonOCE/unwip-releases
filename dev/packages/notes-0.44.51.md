- Your rights: Help › About › Your rights says plainly what you can do with what you make. It's yours, and you can use it anywhere, including in work you sell. Unwip's built-in artwork can be part of it, remixed however you like. What you can't do is sell or give away the built-in artwork on its own (as a symbol pack, brushes, clip art and so on). It's also in Help, in a LICENSES.txt file that comes with Unwip, and on the website.
- Symbol credits: the animal and object symbols from game-icons.net are free to use, even commercially, but their artists ask for a short credit. Any Build piece that uses one now shows the exact credit line with a Copy credit button, for example "Symbols by Lorc, game-icons.net, CC BY 3.0", to put with the picture or on your book's copyright page. The symbol picker says so too.
- Build pictures and maps now only use fonts that come with Unwip (Bookhaven, Oswald, and the new IBM Plex Mono for system-style text), carried inside each picture. A picture looks the same on every device and never depends on the computer's own fonts.
- The writers' quotes on the main screen now come only from writers whose words are free for anyone to use (Austen, Carroll, Twain, Wilde, Thoreau, Johnson, Chesterton, Cather and others).
- Open-source licences lists IBM Plex Mono and the small libraries inside the Windows setup program.
- Build: on a computer, the piece's name has room again instead of being cut to a few letters.
