iPhone and iPad:
- Unwip now tells you when a new version is out. When you come back to it, or every hour, it checks, and if there is one you get "A new version of Unwip is ready" with an Update now button. That saves your writing and reloads. (A Home Screen app is usually woken up rather than restarted, so before this it could keep running an old version.)
- The formatting row above the keyboard no longer hides behind the iPhone's ^ v ✓ bar the first time you tap into the page. It now re-measures for a moment while the keyboard opens.
- The Aa font and size button (from 0.43.14) is on that row, just after H2.
