## Unwip 0.12.2

- **Chapter titles are included in exports again** for books organised in folders. Unwip now works out whether a folder holds chapters (a book or part) or scenes (a "Chapter 3" folder). Right-click a folder → *Documents inside are…* to choose yourself
- New **Chapter titles** choice in Preview & export (and Manuscript style): use the **document names**, the **first line of each chapter** (e.g. "Chapter 2: Are You Talking to Me?") as a proper heading, or add none
- If your chapters already start with a "Chapter…" line, the preview tells you, so they don't show twice
