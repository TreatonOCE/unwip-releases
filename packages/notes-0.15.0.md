## Unwip 0.15: safer with two computers

- **One computer at a time.** Opening a project that is open on your other computer offers to **save and close it there first**. Unwip waits until that is done, then opens it, so the two can never overwrite each other
- **See what changed.** When a chapter was edited on your other computer, the changed words are **highlighted in green** when you open it, with a note of how much changed. Click *Hide highlights* when you are done. The earlier version is kept in Snapshots automatically
- A green dot in the binder marks documents changed elsewhere since you were last here
- **Recently updated** on the main screen shows what you worked on last, and on which computer. Inside a project, the clock button (bottom left) lists the latest changes
