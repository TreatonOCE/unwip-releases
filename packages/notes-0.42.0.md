Conflicted copies, and fixes found by a round of heavy testing:
- New: a Conflicted copies section in the binder. When the same chapter is changed in two places at once (two devices syncing through Dropbox, like Scrivener's conflicted files), the other version goes in there instead of being hidden. Open it to Compare side by side, Use this version, Keep both, or mark it Not needed (it goes to the Trash). The section disappears once it's empty. It works the same on the PC and on the iPhone/iPad version, and syncs between them.
- New: Leave out of the book. Right-click a chapter or folder in the Manuscript to keep it in the binder but out of the preview and every export (it shows faded). Scrivener's "Include in Compile" ticks now come across both ways, so chapters you'd unticked in Scrivener stay out of the book in Unwip, and stay unticked when you go back to Scrivener.
- Find & replace across the book: words split by formatting (a name with one letter in italics, say) are now found and replaced too, ALL-CAPS stays all caps, a chapter open in split view gets the change as well, and Undo no longer throws away anything you typed after the replace.
- Books: chapters titled like "116 - The Market" no longer show the number twice ("Chapter 103: 116 - The Market"), "104 - Interlude: …" counts as an interlude, and e-books number chapters past 99 in the book's own style.
- Backups: besides the last 20, one backup from each of the 14 days before is kept, so a long writing day can't push out every copy from yesterday.
- iPhone/iPad: Dropbox clashes no longer bother you when only things like word counts differ, and a save made while a sync was running can't be overwritten. In Safari, Unwip now reminds you (now and then) that Safari can clear a website's saved data after 7 days without a visit, and how to avoid it: add Unwip to the Home Screen or connect Dropbox.
- Fixed: emptying the Trash (or deleting forever) while a chapter from it was open could lose the last few words typed in it.
- Small: "1 pages" is now "1 page"; toasts with a button fit on a phone screen.
