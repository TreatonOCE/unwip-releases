Build:
- Magic systems now use a scroll icon instead of sparkles.
- If the same piece is changed on two devices before they sync, both versions are kept in Build ("… (iPad’s version)") instead of one being tucked away out of sight. On the PC, Dropbox's own "conflicted copy" files show up as their own piece too.
- A piece that's open on screen reloads when another device changes it, so an older copy is never saved over the newer one.
- Coat of arms: long mottos go on two lines and always fit the ribbon.
- Rune writing: a very long word wraps instead of shrinking the whole picture.
- Phones: tidier magic system rows and spells, the coat of arms title is no longer covered, bigger buttons to tap.

Privacy (Report a problem):
- Reports now remove file paths entirely (they can name you, your computer and your books), keeping only Unwip's own file names, plus email addresses and any bit of writing an error message happens to quote.
- Errors are cleaned when they're noted, so nothing personal is kept on the device either, and Dropbox problems no longer log the name of the file.

Everywhere:
- A very long book title no longer stretches every book card off the side of a phone.
- iPad and iPhone: no more hover bubbles popping up after a tap, the map's ✕ no longer covers its toolbar, the main screen header keeps clear of the notch, and the "blank map" hint goes away when you leave the map.
- Play: the stats wrap on small phones instead of being cut off.
