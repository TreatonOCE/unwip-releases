New: Proof of writing. Show how you wrote your book. Click Record at the bottom right of the writing screen (on a phone: More › Proof of writing).
- Replay (small files): turn it on for a book, and Unwip notes every change you make while writing, with the time: what you typed, deleted and pasted. A long writing day is about 1 MB, kept with the book (it travels through Dropbox like your chapters).
  - Play it back: watch the words appear, at up to 256× speed, with pasted text highlighted. Long pauses are shortened.
  - Save a report: one file anyone can open in a web browser. It shows words typed vs pasted, every paste (when, where and how big), writing time per session, and the replay itself, so a publisher, contest or reader can watch it being written.
  - Make a timelapse video: the replay as a 30-second, 1-minute or 3-minute video, wide (YouTube) or tall (TikTok, Reels, Shorts).
- Screen video (big files): records Unwip's window as a video, like a screen recorder, with a clear warning first about size (up to about 360 MB, 900 MB or 2.25 GB an hour depending on quality), how much free space you have, and a live timer and size while recording. Videos are written straight to disk as they record, in Videos › Unwip, and stop safely if your disk gets nearly full. On iPhone and iPad, use the built-in Screen Recording instead (Unwip tells you how).
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
