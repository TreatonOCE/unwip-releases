## Unwip 0.14

**Pictures in your chapters.** Click the picture button in the toolbar, or just paste or drag a picture in. Click a picture to make it Small, Medium, Large or Full width, line it up left, centre or right, or remove it.
- Big photos are shrunk to a sensible size as they go in, so projects stay light
- Pictures come along in every export: paperback/PDF, Kindle/EPUB, Word and Google Docs
- **Scrivener import** now brings pictures across too: ones inside your text, and picture items in the binder
