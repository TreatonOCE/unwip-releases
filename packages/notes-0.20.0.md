## Unwip 0.20: better maps, and a toolbar that fits small screens

**Maps**
- **Sections:** one map can hold several, such as the whole world, a region close-up and a city. Use **+ Section** to start one empty or generated, and right-click a tab to rename, duplicate, reorder or delete it
- **Generate › Add it to this map:** puts a new island, island chain or continent in the emptiest stretch of sea, keeping everything already there
- **Clear** empties the current section (Undo brings it back)
- **Border tool** (red dotted line) for borders between lands and kingdoms. The river tool is gone: paint waterways with the 🌊 water brush
- **Map frame:** double line, single line, ornate corners with a scale bar, or no frame
- **Brush size** now has a brush icon and shows the size
- **Much smoother painting:** land and water show instantly while you paint, and the full coastline is drawn when you let go. Dragging things around is smoother too

**Toolbar**
- On smaller windows, tools that don't fit go into a **⋯** menu instead of hiding behind Stuck? and Sprint. "Copy for Docs" shortens to "Copy", and Stuck? to its icon, on narrow windows
