- Natural voice for Read aloud (optional): in Read aloud's voice list, pick “Natural voice… (optional download)”. It's Kokoro, a small speech model that only reads your words aloud and can't write, change or suggest anything. It isn't part of Unwip and isn't downloaded unless you ask. It's a one-off download of about 120 MB from its own public sources, each file checked against a known fingerprint, and after that it works offline with nothing you write sent anywhere. There are eight English voices, British and American. The window explains exactly what it is before you download, and you can remove it from the same window. About Unwip, Help and Open-source licences say the same.
- Read aloud prepares the next sentences while it reads. On the PC the voice runs in its own background window and can use several processor cores.
- Find anything (Ctrl+K, the magnifying glass at the top) now puts “Find in this chapter” and “Search the whole book” first when what you type isn't the name of an action or chapter, so you can look for any word there.
