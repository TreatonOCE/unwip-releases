## Unwip 0.16: maps and a welcome setup

- **Map maker.** Right-click a folder (Places is a good spot) and choose *New map here…*. Paint land, or press *Generate* for an island or continent. Then add mountains, forests, towns, rivers, roads and labels. Pins can link to your place sheets. Four styles (parchment, atlas, night, ink), an optional hex grid, and *Export PNG* for your book or website
- **Welcome setup.** Tell Unwip what you wrote with before (Scrivener, Google Docs, Word, Hemingway, or nothing yet) and it sets itself up to feel familiar. You choose the layout, how your book is organised, the colours, the font and a daily goal
- **Example project.** *The Lighthouse Keeper* is a short sample story laid out the way you chose, with a character, a place and a "Start here" note showing where your old program's features are in Unwip
- **Guided tour.** A spotlight walks you round each button and explains what it does, with tips for the program you're coming from
- Run the setup or the tour again any time from *Settings › Help*
