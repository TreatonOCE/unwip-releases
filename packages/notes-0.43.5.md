- iPhone/iPad: groundwork for importing Scrivener projects straight from Dropbox, with no zip: Import › Scrivener › Choose from Dropbox shows your Dropbox (starting in Apps › Scrivener) and imports the project you tap. It uses a separate, read-only Dropbox permission, asked for the first time only. It switches on once Unwip's read-only Dropbox key is added; until then the zip way still works.
