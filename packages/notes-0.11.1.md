## What's new in Unwip 0.11.1

**Typing sounds, rebuilt.** Each one now sounds like the real thing:
- **Typewriter:** a sharp clack, a deep thunk on Space, and the bell and carriage return on Enter
- **Mechanical keyboard:** a crisp double click and a plastic thock
- **Soft keys:** a quiet, muffled laptop tap
- **Pencil:** graphite scratching on paper. Space taps the pencil, Enter draws a line, and Backspace rubs with an eraser

**Bolder letter effects.** Fade, Rise, Drop, Pop, Ink and Typewriter strike are now easy to tell apart.

**Hover for help.** Hover any option, or a little **?**, in Typewriter & animations to see what it does. A new **▶ Demo** button types a sentence for you with your chosen settings.

This is also the first update delivered automatically by Unwip itself.
