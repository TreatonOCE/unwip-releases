New: Build, a third tab next to Write and Play, for the pieces of your world that belong to no single book.
- Maps: the map maker now works on its own. Put a map into any book, linked (fix it once and every book that links it sees the change) or as a separate copy.
- Magic systems: a guided sheet with the questions writers often forget (where the power comes from, what it costs, what it can never do, how the world treats it). Add costs, elements and spells, and set rules like "every spell that uses Mind must pay Memory" or "costs must add up to at least the power". Unwip checks every spell against your rules, and can look through a book for every place a spell, cost or element is mentioned.
- Rune alphabets: draw runes on a dot grid so the whole set looks like one script. Give each one a letter or sound, then type names, mottos and inscriptions and see them written in your runes. Fill A–Z in one go, with random runes to edit if you like.
- Sigils: draw in one slice and it repeats around the circle, mirrored if you like, with rings and an optional ring of your own runes around the edge.
- Coats of arms: shield shapes, divided fields, bands, 19 symbols (or your own runes and sigils), and a motto ribbon. Unwip writes the herald's description as you go and points out where the heralds' rule of colour is broken.
- Copy any of them as a picture, save a PNG, or add it to a book (it goes into Notes or Places).
- On the phone and iPad, Build syncs through Dropbox with everything else.
As always, nothing here is AI: drawing tools, dice and plain rules.
