A big one: everything since 0.44.60.

**Focus, Detail and Lore**
- Three levels instead of two (the buttons above your chapter list, and on the main screen). Focus is a clean page. Detail adds the Outline, Corkboard, Moodboard, Timeline, status labels, synopses, Play and Build. Lore adds character sheets, the Story Bible and Open World. Switching only hides and shows things; nothing in your book changes, and Ctrl+K still finds every tool. Phones go up to Detail.
- Open World (main screen tab at Lore, and a Lore group inside a book) holds magic systems, rune alphabets, sigils and insignia. On a phone, ones made on a bigger screen show in Build as View only.
- Build's LitRPG "Character sheet" is now called Stat system, so it isn't mixed up with the new character sheets.
- The main screen's header stays on two rows at every level and fits smaller windows, with Record and Settings always on screen.

**Character sheets (Lore)**
- Open a character and click Character sheet. Put gear in the slots around the picture, each from the chapter you pick, with a rarity. The timeline at the top steps through the chapters where something changed, so you can look back at what they had and their stats at any point. While looking back, tap the chapter's name to open it; "Back to latest" goes back.
- Stats, skills and items come from the [system messages] in your chapters. "Show stats of" picks whose numbers to show, or "Don't show stats, just gear". Money and shop messages like [New Balance: 1,680] aren't counted as stats.
- Follow a Stat system from Build and the Stats tab checks your chapters' numbers against how the character should grow.
- ‹ and › at the top step to the character before or after without closing the sheet.
- Add to a sheet while you write: select an item's name and choose Add to sheet. Make a new sheet from Open World or the Character sheets list.
- Sheets sync between devices. On a phone they open view only.

**Story Bible (Lore)**
- One for each series, or for a book on its own: People, Places and Things from every book, with where each one appears, who they're often with and their gear. Pages can be shared between books in a series.
- Select words in a chapter and choose Add to bible to pin them as a fact, a plot thread or a glossary word. "As of" shows the bible up to any chapter, with nothing from later on.
- Relationships on every page, each able to start and stop at a chapter, and a Web tab that draws them as a map.
- Threads shows what you've set up and how long it's been waiting for a pay-off. Glossary checks the spelling of your world's words in every chapter.
- Put books in order with the ‹ › arrows, take one out of the series, delete it, or end the series. Show people All together or By book.
- Export… saves it as Word or PDF, up to a chapter if you like. On a phone it's in Build and read only.

**Writing**
- Autocorrect as you type, with no AI: common typos (teh, adn, recieve), "i" on its own and a missing capital at the start of a sentence. Add your own corrections (right-click › Always correct to, or Ctrl+K). Optional one-letter-off fixes that leave names alone. Backspace or Ctrl+Z straight away undoes one. Change it all in Settings › Autocorrect.
- **Add to…** on words you pick: put them on a character, place or thing page, under a heading, or start a timeline event linked to the chapter.
- Spark has a new tool, Interview a character, with question sets for main characters, villains, love interests and more, or your own questions. Its pages go below Trash, ready to drag into place, and Spark no longer glows while you type in the split window.
- Find (Ctrl+F) looks only in your chapter, keeps your typing as you backspace, and Enter goes to the next match.
- Choosing an icon: type a word to find an emoji (trash, dragon, castle).
- Turning a list of abilities written as [system messages] into sheets makes one sheet per ability.
- Fonts: Lora and Libre Baskerville no longer show as favourites, since Unwip has them as Larkspur and Bookhaven. If some text keeps its own font when you change the Style font, Unwip tells you and offers Font for the whole book.
- The side panel button opens Insights, Comments, Changes, Snapshots and Style. Revise has its own button.
- Calmer wording across the app, and the typewriter bell sounds like a real bell.

**LitRPG and exports**
- "Copy for Docs" and its arrow are now one Export button, with a grouped menu. Ctrl+Shift+C still copies for Docs.
- Copy for Royal Road has more looks for system message boxes, and "Matched to each message", which picks a look per box (warnings red, rewards gold, skills purple and so on).
- A short message on its own, like [I am], can be a full box, a box that fits the words, or bold brackets with no box (Settings › LitRPG). The Kindle / EPUB preview now shows the boxes too.
- No hard feelings: a book imported from Scrivener can go back out exactly as Scrivener had it, folders, order, names and icons included (for books imported from now on).
- Scrivener import no longer warns about deleted documents in Scrivener's Trash.
- Import › a folder that is an Unwip book now brings it in as a book.

**Build and maps**
- Symbols in categories with a search, one colour row everywhere with "From picture", size and turn handles on coats of arms, sigils and insignia, four ribbon shapes for mottos, and a proper parchment scroll for magic systems.
- Maps: nothing cut off at the edges, more zoom out, clearer block fill on towns, Random town adds on top instead of replacing, a Select an area tool, and fields, woods and buildings can be turned and resized. Label boxes fit their lettering, and the brush circle follows the mouse.
- "+ Map or picture" links a map or other Build piece to a character, place or thing page.
- The world calendar is redone: a year strip with seasons and full moons, and easy month lengths and week days.
- Fixed: Copy picture on the PC, and tapping a small building on a phone resizing it.

**Updates, saving and the rest**
- The PC and Mac find updates sooner (every hour, and when you come back after a while), keep a "Restart to update" button until you restart, and Settings › Updates says when it last checked.
- Settings › What's new shows the latest three versions, and a new version shows what it brings.
- Book cards show when each book was last changed, with the time.
- Screen videos are saved as MP4 where possible, and one cut off part-way still plays.
- Your settings carry over between versions, and books sync safely between this version and the last one.
- Fixed: on the web, iPhone and iPad, changing several settings quickly could lose some of them.
- Many smaller fixes, and over 30 older fixes now have an automatic check each, so they can't quietly come undone.
