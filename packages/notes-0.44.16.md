New: Build from inside a book, and lots of fixes.
- Build without leaving your chapter: a Build button at the top of a book (Build on phones, or Ctrl/Cmd+K) opens your maps, magic systems, runes, sigils and coats of arms, or starts a new one. On a PC or Mac each opens in its own window to keep beside your chapter; on phones and iPads it opens over the chapter with "Back to the book".
- Coats of arms: symbols always fit inside the shield (no more cut-off stars on diamond or round shields). Choose an arrangement (in a row, in a column, slanting, one then two, around the edge), change their size, or pick "Where I put them" and drag them anywhere.
- Magic system: a short plain-words guide at the top explains what the page is for and each part, which are now numbered and explained more simply.
- Typing effects now show in the "Try it here" box, each one looks clearly different, and there are three new ones: Glow, Spin and Scatter.
- The Record button is plain until something is actually recording (red only while recording), so it doesn't look like it's already on.
- iPhone and iPad: settings windows no longer pop a drop-down open by themselves, and the keyboard stays down unless there's just one box to type in.
