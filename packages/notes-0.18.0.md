## Unwip 0.18: real book typesetting

The paperback preview and print PDF now set your book the way a printed novel is set, page by page.

- **Six book designs:** Classic, Fantasy, Literary, Romance, Thriller and Modern. Each has its own chapter openings ("Chapter One" with an ornament, a big numeral, small capitals and so on), fonts, drop capitals and scene-break ornaments
- **Proper book pages:** mirrored margins with an inside gutter sized for your page count (as KDP asks), chapters starting on a right-hand page, running heads (author on the left, title on the right), page numbers, and no heads on chapter-opening or blank pages
- **Front and back matter:** half-title, title page (with subtitle and publisher), copyright page, dedication, epigraph, a contents page with real page numbers, acknowledgements, about the author and "also by". Fill them in under *Title page, copyright & more…*
- **Parts** get their own page; "Prologue" and "Epilogue" aren't numbered as chapters
- **Fine control:** trim size (including Royal and B-format), text size, line spacing, text and heading fonts, scene-break style, drop caps, small-caps first lines, justification, hyphenation, large print
- **Print-ready PDF:** fonts fully embedded as printers require. What you see in the preview is exactly what prints, page for page
- **The e-book matches:** the EPUB now has the same chapter headings, drop caps, scene breaks, title page, copyright page, dedication, epigraph and back matter
- **Six book fonts included with Unwip** (EB Garamond, Libre Baskerville, Crimson Pro, Lora, Cinzel, Oswald). They also appear in your font lists
