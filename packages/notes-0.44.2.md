Opening a book, the safe way (like Scrivener):
- Phone and iPad: a book another device has changed shows "⚠ Changed on another device" on the main screen. Opening it brings those changes in first (there's an "Open without waiting" button if you're in a hurry), so you never start writing in an old copy.
- No internet (airplane mode, no signal): opening a book in Dropbox warns that changes from your other devices may not be here yet, with Open anyway or Cancel. It asks once per book until you're back online.
- PC and Mac: the same warning when the computer is offline, or when the Dropbox or OneDrive app isn't running (since that app is what keeps the book in step there).
- If the sync before opening can't finish, Unwip says so and lets you choose, rather than opening quietly.
