Getting ready for Unwip on Mac:
- Unwip now knows when it's running on a Mac: a proper Mac menu bar (so Cmd+C, Cmd+V, Cmd+Z and Cmd+Q work), shortcuts shown as ⌘K instead of Ctrl+K, "This Mac" instead of "This PC", "Show in Finder", and Dropbox, OneDrive and iCloud Drive found where a Mac keeps them.
- A few shortcuts now also answer to Cmd (paste as plain text, dialogue colours, the comment and footnote shortcuts).
- Nothing changes on Windows, iPhone or iPad.
