Fixes (Windows):
- The taskbar now calls Unwip "Unwip", with its icon, instead of "Electron" (the name of the engine Unwip runs on).
- Opening Unwip when it's already open (from the taskbar, the Start menu or a shortcut) now brings back the window you have, instead of starting a second, empty-looking copy. Two copies working on the same books could have saved over each other.
- To have a chapter in a window of its own, right-click it in the binder › Open in its own window.
