- Dialogue view (Ctrl+Shift+D, or search “Dialogue”): fades everything except the dialogue so you can read just the conversation. Switch to Narration to fade the dialogue instead. Pick a character to see only their lines (Unwip guesses who's speaking from the names on your Characters sheets, like “said Maz”, including first-person narrators). Tick “Only these paragraphs” to hide the rest. Shows how much of the chapter is dialogue. Nothing on the page changes.
- Writing history (Goals › Full history, the main screen, or search “History”): a calendar of every day you wrote across all your books, with streaks, best day, average on a writing day, words per month and per book, time spent writing, and the times of day and days of the week your words come. Click a day to see what you wrote. Time of day and time spent are counted from this version on.
- Series bible (search “Series bible”, or right-click Characters): books in the same series share their Characters, Places and Items sheets. A shared sheet is in every book of the series (marked with a little books icon) and changes in one book reach the others. If a sheet was changed in two books, the newer one wins and the other is kept as a snapshot. Sheets with the same name in two books are joined up, with the old text kept as a snapshot.
