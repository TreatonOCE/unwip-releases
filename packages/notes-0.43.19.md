Fixes:
- Importing Markdown (Obsidian and others): formatting written as HTML tags inside the Markdown now comes across as formatting, so <u>underline</u>, <b>bold</b>, <i>italic</i>, <s>strikethrough</s>, superscript, subscript and line breaks work alongside the usual *italics* and **bold**. Other tags, like coloured <span>s, are dropped but their words kept.
