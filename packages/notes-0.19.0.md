## Unwip 0.19: grammar checking

Revise has three new kinds of highlight. All of them are rule-based and work offline, with no AI, and each comes with a one-click fix.

- **Grammar:** "could of" → could have, "a apple" → an apple, "they was" → they were, "between you and I", "lets go" → let's go, a lowercase "i", "supposed to" and "used to". It also checks dialogue punctuation ("Stop." she said → "Stop," she said) and questions in dialogue that are missing their question mark
- **Commonly confused words:** its / it's, your / you're, their / there / they're, then / than, lose / loose, affect / effect, whose / who's, to / too, breath / breathe, peek / peak, past / passed, led / lead, advice / advise, accept / except, whether / weather, quiet / quite
- **UK / US spelling mix:** flags a chapter that uses both "colour" and "color", "grey" and "gray", "realise" and "realize" and so on, and suggests your usual spelling

Characters' dialogue is left alone for grammar like "he don't" and "we was", because people talk that way on purpose. On a real 133,000-word novel, the checker raised about 25 highlights, nearly all of them genuine.
