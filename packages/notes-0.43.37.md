Getting started:
- The welcome setup is two screens instead of seven. Pick what you've written with before (Scrivener, Docs, Word, Obsidian…) and Unwip sets the layout, font and book structure to match, then gets your book in: bring it in, try the Dracula example, or start a blank book.
- Want to choose those yourself? "Change layout, font or colours…" on the first screen opens the detailed choices.
