- iPhone/iPad: importing from Scrivener now works with a zip of the project. Apple doesn't let web apps open a .scriv folder (which is why the picker kept downloading the project, making a numbered copy, and still wouldn't let you choose it). In the Files app, press and hold the .scriv project › Compress, then in Unwip choose Import › Scrivener › Choose the zip. Unwip explains the steps when you start.
