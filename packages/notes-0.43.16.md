Fonts on the iPhone and iPad:
- The Aa font list now shows the fonts your iPhone or iPad actually has (Palatino, Baskerville, Hoefler Text, Didot, Optima, Snell Roundhand, Zapfino, Avenir and more), grouped as Your favourites, Unwip's book fonts, Imported and On this device. Windows-only fonts it can't show are left out.
- Palatino works across devices: Windows calls it Palatino Linotype and Apple calls it Palatino. Text set in it on the PC now shows in Palatino on the iPhone, and the other way round, and Word files get the name Windows knows.
- A font only your iPhone has (Snell Roundhand, say) shows as a stand-in on the PC, since Windows doesn't come with it.
